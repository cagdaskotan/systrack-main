Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about SysTrack.

Which targets can be monitored?

ICMP, HTTP/HTTPS, and service checks are available for IP, hostname, or URL targets.

Which notification channels are available?

SysTrack currently supports Email and Telegram channels.

How are SLA reports calculated?

Based on availability and the selected time range.

What does service discovery do?

It lists services on a target and helps add them to monitoring.
